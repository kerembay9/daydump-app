What changes

  • Gap and Corners share one row as steppers: Gap in steps of 2 (0 to 12), Corners in steps of 4 (0 to 16). They show only on Split, Bento and Grid.
  • Matte is gone from Fit. 1 Photo is Fill or Blur fill, so it has no frame controls besides Fit. The colour row is now Behind and shows only when something is behind the photos: a gap, or Scrapbook prints.
  • Strength is Subtle 35 · Medium 68 · Strong 100. One tap, no dragging.
  • Adjust keeps the tool circles (they already show each value) and swaps the ruler for a stepper: ±10 a tap, hold to repeat, Reset to 0. Auto still sets them all at once.
  • Texture goes from three slider rows to one row of three chips. Each tap moves Off → Low → Medium → High → Off.
