What's new on this tab

  • Layout | Photos splits the studio by what a control acts on. Both tabs keep the canvas and the filmstrip, so you never lose your place.
  • Arrangement per slide. Switch to This slide to give one slide its own layout; its counter gets a •. Strip (3 rows) is new.
  • Frame moves into Edit: Fit (Fill / Matte / Blur fill), Gap, Corners and the matte colour. Today Fit is read-only from Profile.
  • Grid guide on slide 1 at 4:5 shows the 3:4 area your profile grid keeps.
  • Cell toolbar: Edit photo, Replace (a similar shot), Swap (then tap the other photo).
  • Swipe or tap the chevrons to change slides; a tap on the matte or a gap does too (left third back, the rest forward).
