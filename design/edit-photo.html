Edit studio · Photos

The second tab of the Edit studio. Layout sets the dump: ratio, arrangement, gap, matte and crop. Photos changes the pixels: a Look for the whole dump, Adjust for one photo, and Texture for the film feel. Tap a photo to edit it alone, and hold the canvas to see the originals. Every phone below is the same screen in a different starting state, and all of them work. Everything here is a Skia colour matrix, shader or blend on the phone; the suggestions come from scores Tidy already computes.

Look · All photos. This is the view when you open the tab: one look on every photo. The tiles preview your own photo, not a colour dot. Tap the active look again to find Strength.

Adjust · This photo. When you tap a photo, it gets its own edits and the rest dim. Tidy already knows the second photo is dark, so the fix is one tap. Drag the ruler. It clicks at 0.

Texture. Grain, Fade and Vignette come bundled with each look and follow the scope chip. A rust dot in the strip marks a photo with its own edits (the fourth one is on B&W Roll).
