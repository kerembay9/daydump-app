Today, play apart from keeping

Two directions. In both, the bingo card becomes a taped paper card with the camera notched into its top edge. A keeps one page: the side quest becomes a sticker stuck on the card's corner, and the history shrinks to a clothesline of the week's prints and a strip of stickers. B splits it into two tabs: Play fits on one screen without scrolling, and Album has room to grow, with a month of bingo prints, a streak, and the full sticker sheet. I'd go with B: past bingos need their own space, and Play stops being a long scroll.
