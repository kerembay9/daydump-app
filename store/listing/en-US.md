## Description
Your camera roll already holds your next photo dump. PicDump finds the best shots from your days, weeks and trips, picks 4 to 10 photos for each, and lays them out ready to post.

Everything runs on your phone. Your photos never leave it. No account, no ads, no tracking.

FIND YOUR DUMPS
• Suggested dumps built from your own photos: "A week in Seattle", "A red winter", "Cinnamon rolls".
• Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly recaps that are always free to open.
• Search your photos by describing them, in your own language.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Add or swap photos, see the pacing, pick a layout and frame.
• Crop each slide with a drag and a pinch.
• Film looks, grain, fade and borders.

POST IN A TAP
• Save the slides to a PicDump album and open Instagram or TikTok, or use the share sheet.
• Drafts and posted dumps live in your Library. Reuse one to start a new dump.

CLEAN UP
• Tidy finds screenshots, receipts and near-duplicates so you can clear the clutter in a few taps. Undo and restore are always free.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• The photo index, suggestions and search are built on your phone.
• "Hide sensitive photos" keeps IDs, cards and papers out of your dumps.

PICDUMP PRO
Free: see every suggested dump, open the recaps, and unlock one full dump a week. Pro opens every dump and search, adds all film looks and textures, lets you move Tidy's picks to Trash, sets 4 to 20 photos per dump, and removes the end slide.

PicDump Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, weekly or yearly. Prices are shown in the app before you buy. Payment is charged to your store account at confirmation. The subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel it in your store account settings.
